One small routine, four useful steps.

01 / CHECK IN

Start with how you feel.

Add a feeling and severity. Include pressure context when available, or save without pressure or location.

02 / EXPLORE TRENDS

Look for your patterns.

Explore recorded days, pressure groups and changes over time. Trends describe associations in your records, without claiming that pressure caused a symptom.

03 / ASK ATMOSFIX

Make sense of your data.

Ask what stands out and see an explanation grounded in your records. On-device intelligence is available on supported devices and configurations.

04 / CHOOSE ALERTS

Feel more prepared.

Choose alerts for high, low or rapid pressure changes, with the thresholds and locations that matter to you. Notifications require system permission.

At home across Apple devices.

Live readings and forecasts

Live barometric readings use the sensor on supported iPhone and iPad hardware while the app is active. Weather pressure forecasts provide a different, sea-level-adjusted view, including on Mac.

Quick glances and actions

Widgets, Live Activities, App Intents and Siri integration bring useful pressure information and actions closer on supported platforms.

On your wrist

The Apple Watch companion and complications keep pressure information within easy reach.

A native experience

A Liquid Glass interface, a native Mac app and optional iCloud check-in sync help the routine fit into your day. Saved places and alert preferences stay on each device.
